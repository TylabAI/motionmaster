# Community guidelines

[English](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) · [简体中文](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.zh-CN.md)

We want the MotionMaster community to remain professional, welcoming, and specific.

- Respect differences in experience, background, and perspective.
- Discuss facts, reproduction steps, and technical tradeoffs.
- Do not harass, shame, discriminate against, or expose another person's private information.
- Do not upload videos, captions, credentials, or personal data that you are not authorized to publish in Issues, Discussions, or examples.
- Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove content that violates these guidelines and may restrict repeated violations.

Use GitHub private vulnerability reporting or a private contact published by the maintainers for matters that should not be handled publicly.
